Set up for success
8 daily rules built around your businesses and your life
👗
RULE 01
Get dressed every day
Even on post-shift days. You're a CEO, not a patient.
🔥
RULE 02
Do the hardest task first
The call you dread — do it first. Rest of the day feels easy.
🎧
RULE 03
Feed your mind every morning
Podcast, sermon, audiobook. Intentional input shapes output.
🪑
RULE 04
Dedicated workspace
One spot — clean, yours, set up the way you like.
⏱️
RULE 05
Time block — one business at a time
🔵 SSS · 🟡 STS · 🟠 AITG. In a block, nothing else exists.
📋
RULE 06
Write 1 visible task per business
Finish it completely before starting anything else.
📆
RULE 07
Follow your home schedule
You built it — trust it. The plan removes the guesswork.
🌙
RULE 08
Protect your sleep like a business asset
After a shift, sleep IS the job. DND on. No business.
